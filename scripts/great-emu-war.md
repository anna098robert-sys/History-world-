## 2. Full voiceover script

> I joined an army… and we lost a war. To birds.
>
> Australia, 1932. Twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat.
>
> So the army sent soldiers. With machine guns. And me. I carried the snacks.
>
> Our commander, Major Meredith, had a plan. Find the emus. Fire. Win.
>
> Day one. We found them. We fired. They ran… in every direction. Like they'd rehearsed it.
>
> Then: the perfect ambush. About a thousand emus walked straight toward us. We waited… we fired… and the gun jammed.
>
> Okay. New plan. Put the machine gun on a truck! Genius!
>
> The emus were faster. The road was so bumpy, nobody could aim.
>
> After about a month, the army did the math. Nearly ten thousand bullets. Around a thousand emus. That's about ten bullets per bird.
>
> The Major said that with an army of soldiers as tough as these birds, he could face any army in the world.
>
> So the soldiers packed up and went home. The emus stayed.
>
> The farmers asked the army for help again. Three more times. The army said: no thanks.
>
> And me? One emu walked up, looked me in the eye… and ate my snacks. I surrendered. Officially, I'm the only prisoner of the Great Emu War.
>

## 4. Final twist

The army really did **lose to the emus**:
- **Withdrawal:** after about five weeks of fighting, the soldiers were pulled out.
- **Farmers refused:** farmers asked for military help again in 1934, 1943 and 1948, and were turned down every time.

The fictional comic button on top: the doodle guy becomes the war's "only prisoner" after an emu eats his snacks and he surrenders with a napkin.
